## Defense Starts Before the Hitter Swings

At high levels of indoor volleyball, a spiked ball crosses from the net to the back court in **0.25 to 0.35 seconds**—faster than human visual reaction time alone allows for full lateral movement. Great liberos and back-row defenders win rallies by reading three pre-contact cues:

1. **Set Location Relative to the Antenna**: A tight set inside the antenna invites a sharp cross-court cut; a set pushed wide past the antenna narrows the hitter's line window.
2. **Hitter Approach Angle & Shoulder Axis**: Watching the hitter's non-hitting guide hand and hip rotation reveals whether they are opening up to power cross or rotating late down the line.
3. **Stepping Out of the Block Shadow**: Once the front-row double block seals a seam, the Zone 5 and Zone 1 defenders must adjust one step outside the block's visual shadow and execute a balanced **split-step** at the exact instant of contact.
