## The Physics of the Penultimate Step

An elite outside or opposite hitter doesn't jump high merely from concentric quad strength—they convert horizontal approach speed into vertical lift through the **penultimate step braking impulse** and **block foot plant**.

- **Step 1 (Directional Timing Step)**: Establishes rhythm relative to the setter's touch.
- **Step 2 (Acceleration Step)**: Builds horizontal momentum toward the court attack zone.
- **Step 3 (Penultimate Step)**: Long, flat, low-center-of-mass stride with aggressive heel-to-toe braking impulse while both arms swing back behind the torso.
- **Step 4 (Block Foot Plant)**: Angled 30°–45° inward toward the setter to arrest forward drift into the net and redirect kinetic energy vertically.
