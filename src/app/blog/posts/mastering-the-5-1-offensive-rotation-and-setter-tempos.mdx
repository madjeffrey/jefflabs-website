## Why the 5-1 Remains the Gold Standard

In a **5-1 offensive system**, a single setter orchestrates the offense across all six rotations—operating as a back-row setter with **three front-row attackers** in three rotations (P1, P6, P5) and as a front-row setter with **two front-row attackers** plus a back-row Opposite/Pipe option in the other three (P4, P3, P2).

## Understanding Offensive Tempo

Modern volleyball offense is built on **tempo**—synchronizing the hitter's approach steps with the setter's hands to beat the opposing middle blocker:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="1st Tempo (51 / Quick / 31 Shoot)" defaultOpen={true}>
    The middle blocker is already airborne or planting their penultimate step **as the ball touches the setter's hands**. This forces the opposing middle blocker to commit before reading the set direction, opening 1-on-1 seams for the pin hitters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="2nd Tempo (Hut / Go / Back-Row Pipe & D-Ball)">
    The hitter takes their first directional step as the pass peaks, contacting a fast, flat trajectory at the antenna or out of Zone 6 (Pipe) before the double block can fully close the seam.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="3rd Tempo (High Out-of-System Ball)">
    Used when a pass is pushed off the net (1-rating pass). High apex gives the outside hitter or opposite time to adjust their footwork and tool the outside hand of a set block.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Rotational Overlap Rules in Code

In SpikeFlow, we verify whether a serve-receive formation is legal by checking each player's coordinates against their adjacent front/back and left/right neighbors at the moment of service contact:

    {
      language: "ts",
      label: "src/tactics/overlapCheck.ts",
      code: `export type PositionSlot = "P1" | "P2" | "P3" | "P4" | "P5" | "P6";
export type Coord = { x: number; y: number }; // y=0 net, y=9 endline

export function isRotationLegal(pos: Record<PositionSlot, Coord>): string[] {
  const errors: string[] = [];
  if (pos.P4.y >= pos.P5.y) errors.push("Overlap: P4 (LF) must be in front of P5 (LB)");
  if (pos.P3.y >= pos.P6.y) errors.push("Overlap: P3 (MF) must be in front of P6 (MB)");
  if (pos.P2.y >= pos.P1.y) errors.push("Overlap: P2 (RF) must be in front of P1 (RB)");
  return errors;
}`
