## Overview

**NetPulse** combines high-frame-rate smartphone video with biomechanical impulse-momentum modeling to track an athlete's approach jump height, ground contact time on the penultimate plant step, and arm-swing contact point.

## Key Features

- **Flight-Time & Center-of-Mass Jump Calculation**: Combines hip keypoint displacement with takeoff-to-landing flight duration for accurate vertical jump tracking without force plates.
- **Synchronized Video & Telemetry Playback**: Scrubbing through the approach steps highlights knee flexion angles and block-jump reactivity in real time.
- **Progressive Overload Load Management**: Tracks total high-intensity jump volume per practice to prevent patellar tendinopathy ("jumper's knee").
