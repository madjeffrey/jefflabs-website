## Overview

Teaching rotational overlap rules and serve-receive transitions in a **5-1 offensive system** on a static whiteboard often leaves players confused about when they can release to their base positions. **SpikeFlow** brings the tactical clipboard to life with smooth state transitions, overlap validation, and an integrated tournament bracket engine.

## Key Features

- **Live Overlap Validator**: Automatically checks FIVB/Volleyball Canada rotational legality (front-to-back and left-to-right positional constraints) before the serve is contacted.
- **Phase State Machine**: Smoothly animates all 6 players across four rally phases: *Serve Receive Stack*, *Setter Release & Approach*, *Base Defense (Perimeter/Rotational)*, and *Transition Counter-Attack*.
- **Real-Time Pool Play & Bracket Scoring**: Mobile-first courtside scorekeeper that calculates set/point differentials and updates elimination brackets instantly.

## Technologies Used

- **Next.js App Router & TypeScript**: Strongly-typed state machines for rotations P1 through P6.
- **Tailwind CSS**: Hardware-accelerated court transitions and responsive mobile scoreboards.
- **Supabase Realtime**: Synchronizes live court scores and tactical board states across coaching staff devices.

## Outcome

Adopted by local club and adult competitive squads to onboard new players into 5-1 and 6-2 systems in half the practice time.
