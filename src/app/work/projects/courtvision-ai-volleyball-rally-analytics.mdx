## Overview

In competitive indoor volleyball, rallies unfold in fractions of a second—a jump topspin serve crosses the 9-meter court in under 0.65 seconds. **CourtVision AI** is a full-stack computer vision and tactical telemetry platform built to turn single-angle baseline video into actionable court analytics for coaches and athletes.

## Key Features

- **120 FPS Ball Trajectory & Serve Speed Estimation**: Fine-tuned object detection model tracks high-velocity serves and spikes across net occlusion, homographically projecting pixel coordinates onto the standard 18m × 9m volleyball court grid.
- **Automated Setter Distribution Heatmaps**: Classifies 1st-tempo quick sets (A/B/C), 2nd-tempo combinations, pipe attacks (Zone 6), and pin sets (Zones 4 & 2) by pass rating (0–3 scale).
- **Pose-Guided Vertical Jump & Block Timing**: Extracts ankle, hip, and shoulder keypoints to measure penultimate step velocity, peak attack touch height, and block seal timing.
- **Interactive Next.js + Tailwind Dashboard**: Allows players to filter rallies by rotation (P1–P6), side-out phase, or hitter tendency with instant video clip synchronization.

  if (pt.xMeters < 3) return isFrontRow ? 4 : 5;

## Technologies Used

- **PyTorch & OpenCV**: Custom high-speed volleyball detection, Kalman filtering for occluded frames at the net tape, and 2D-to-3D court homography calibration.
- **Next.js & TypeScript**: Server-rendered match reports and low-latency interactive court heatmaps.
- **Tailwind CSS**: Custom dark-court glassmorphic interface designed for sideline tablet review between sets.

## Outcome

Reduced post-match video tagging time from 3 hours per match to under 12 minutes while giving hitters and liberos objective biomechanics feedback on serve-receive seams and attack angles.
